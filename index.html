About

I'm an educational strategist with over a decade of experience leading digital transformation in higher education. I combine expertise in instructional design, AI adoption, and technology ecosystems to create accessible, student-centered learning environments.

Currently serving as Assistant Provost for Innovations in Learning, Teaching, and Technology at Quinnipiac University, I lead initiatives that prepare learners and institutions for a rapidly changing world. My work spans AI strategy and governance, learning design, faculty development, and enterprise technology systems.

I believe deeply in the power of thoughtful design to create equitable access to education. Whether I'm consulting on AI integration, leading an LMS migration, or teaching graduate students, I'm always asking: how can we do this better for the humans involved?
